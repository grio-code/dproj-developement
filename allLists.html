Every punch list in your workspace. Group them into folders. A folder that holds a project's list joins that project.

Shared saving isn't set up yet, so lists are saved on this phone only. Add your Firebase settings to firebase-starter/firebase-config.js to share them.

Tip: press and hold a list or a folder, then drag it onto a folder.

    Put it on your iPhone: in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on.
